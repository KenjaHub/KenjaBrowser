DEEP-SEA EXPLORATION

Deep-Sea Robots Map the Ocean Floor in Real Time

By Mira Tanaka · Senior Science Correspondent · 6 min read
LIVE CC · EN → JA
We are seeing the seafloor for the first time
海底を初めてリアルタイムで見ている
04:28 / 08:19

A fleet of autonomous underwater vehicles has begun streaming high-resolution maps of the abyssal plain, covering in a week what earlier expeditions needed a decade to survey.

The project's engineers say the milestone opens a new era for ocean science — one in which the deep ocean is observed continuously rather than in brief, costly expeditions.
