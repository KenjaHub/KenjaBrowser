Privacy Policy

KenjaBrowser — KofukuAI · Last updated: August 2026

KenjaBrowser processes all speech recognition, translation, and browsing data entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We have no analytics SDKs and no backend.

  • Audio captions and translations never leave your Mac.
  • The free-tier quota counter is stored locally in your app preferences.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details or identity.
  • Website content you visit is loaded directly by the WebKit engine; the built-in content blocker only applies local filter rules.

The app requests macOS permissions solely to provide captions: a one-time Screen & System Audio Recording grant that covers only this app's own audio. No system-wide capture, no screen content, and no microphone input is ever recorded.

Contact: support@kofukuai.com
